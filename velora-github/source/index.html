A few little questions.

Not sure about something? Search below or just ask me. No pressure, promise.

Are these prices final?

The price you see is the starting price for the style you pick. Usage, add-ons and extra details are added on top, and the form shows a live estimate. Custom requests have no fixed price, so I'll send you a quote. The final price is always confirmed with you before work starts.

How do I commission you?

Click a service, fill in the form and press “Review and send”. Then continue to Ko-fi: your request is copied for you, so paste it into the Ko-fi message. I'll confirm availability, queue and the final price with you.

What's the difference between halfbody, fullbody and chibi?

Halfbody covers the upper body, fullbody covers the whole character from head to toe, and chibi is a small, cute, stylized version. Each style has its own price. Not sure which one fits? Tell me how you plan to stream and I'll help you pick.

What does each usage option mean?

Personal is for your own use. Commercial: content covers streaming and content creation. Both are included in the price. Commercial: merchandising is for selling items that use the art and adds $200. If you're unsure which one applies, ask me before you order.

What do the add-ons include?

VBridger is +$300, Outfit is +$150, Toggle is +$30, Expression is +$20 and Hairstyle is +$50. Outfit, Toggle, Expression and Hairstyle can be ordered in any quantity. Use the + and − buttons in the form and the estimate updates right away. We'll confirm the exact features of each add-on when we plan your project.

Can I ask for something that isn't listed?

Yes. Choose Custom Request and describe your idea. There's no fixed price for custom requests, so I'll review your idea and send you a quote.

I have a deadline. Can you make it?

Tell me in the “hard deadline” field of the form. I'll check my queue and let you know honestly whether it's possible, before you commit to anything.

Can I split the payment?

Payments go through Ko-fi. Payment terms, including whether the payment can be split, are discussed and confirmed with me before work starts. Custom requests are paid only after I've sent you a quote.

May you share my commission publicly?

The form asks whether I may stream my work on your model or share it with credit. Answer Yes, No or Ask me first, and I'll follow it.

What should I send as a character reference?

A link to images of your character is perfect (Google Drive, Imgur, Pinterest and similar). Clear front views, outfit details and any pose or expression ideas help me the most.
