Commission Flow

From Discuss to Finish

01. Discussion

Contact Velora to discuss their model, rigging requirements, pricing, and estimated turnaround time.

02. Order

Submit commission request through VGen, with the scope, pricing, and terms clearly agreed upon.

03. Payment & Order Confirmation

Completes payment through VGen Payment & Paypal. Work begins once payment and order details are confirmed.

04. Queue

The commission enters Velora’s queue and is scheduled according to the current workload and estimated timeline.

05. Production

Velora works on the project and provides progress updates to the client.

06. Delivery

Client reviews the result, eligible revisions are completed according to the agreed terms, and the final files are delivered.
